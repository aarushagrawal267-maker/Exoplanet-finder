Exoplanet Finder results viewer: the full app runs locally

Held-out test results of the four models trained by the application on real NASA Kepler data. Everything on this page was computed by the local app and exported from its database; no model runs here.

A planet-like score is a research lead, not a discovery. The models rank how much a transit-like signal resembles signals that NASA vetting labelled planet candidates. Confirming a planet needs independent follow-up (centroid analysis, imaging, radial velocities, statistical validation).

About

The application downloads NASA's Kepler catalogue and long-cadence light curves, builds star-separated training/validation/test splits, trains logistic regression, a random forest, a single-view and a two-view (AstroNet-style) CNN, and evaluates each final model once on the test stars. This page publishes one such run; it is a viewer, not the application. Install the app (Windows, macOS or Linux) from the project repository to explore the catalogue, analyse light curves, train models and score any signal. Larger runs are documented in the repository's reference results (Standard and Full tiers).
